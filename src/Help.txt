VARIABLES
horses = 25           name first
25"horses"            value first; same assignment
(20 + 5)"horses"      expressions work too
3"teabage" + 2 =      5; teabage is 3
3"tea" + 2"sugar"     define several values in one line
(3 + 2)"total"        name a whole calculation
horses * 3            → 75

a = 3
v = 2
a + v                 → 5
total = a * v         → 6
ans + 10              → last successful result + 10

Variables are local to each note, ignore case, and are
evaluated from top to bottom. Edit an earlier value and
all later results update. Use letters, digits and _ in
names; start with a letter or _. Constants and ans are
reserved. Forward references are not supported.
Quoted names bind the preceding value, including its sign
and powers. Use parentheses to name a sum or product.
You can reuse a name later in the same expression.
Failed lines discard bindings and invalidate reassignments.

NOTES & WRITTEN ANSWERS
Type cls or clear on its own line, then press Enter
to clear the current note and its variables. Commands
ignore case and surrounding spaces. Ctrl+Z undoes it.
Other notes and settings are kept.

# A comment, heading or reminder
price = 48 # an inline memo
Plain sentences can be memos too.
a + v =               → 5
a + v = 5             → checks the written answer
a + v = 9             → shows correction: → 5
Use # before memos that contain numbers or operators.
Generated answers appear inline after = without being
inserted into your editable text. Click one to copy it,
or use Ctrl+Shift+C. Plain a = 3 is not duplicated.

ARITHMETIC
+  -  *  /            add, subtract, multiply, divide
% or mod              remainder: 17 % 5 → 2
^ or **               power: 2^3 → 8
( )                   grouping: (2 + 3) * 4 → 20
2pi, 2(3 + 4)         implicit multiplication
5!                    factorial → 120
1.25e3                scientific notation → 1250
Percentages: 200 * 15 / 100 → 30
Multiplication and division have equal precedence.
Powers associate right; -2^2 = -4.

floor(2.8)            round down → 2

DEG / RAD controls trig inputs and inverse-trig results.
Constants: pi (π), tau, e, phi (φ / golden ratio).

BITWISE
6 & 3 or 6 and 3      AND → 2
6 | 3 or 6 or 3       OR → 7
6 xor 3               XOR → 5
~6 or not 6           NOT → -7
3 << 2, 12 >> 2       shifts → 12, 3
0xff, 0b1010          hexadecimal / binary literals
Bitwise inputs must be exact integers, up to 2^53−1
in magnitude. Shifts accept 0–52. ^ means power.

Notes menu reopens closed notes. To permanently delete a note,
open it and choose Notes > Delete this note, then confirm.
Deletion removes its saved backup too and cannot be undone.
 Settings changes the
theme, font size and background opacity per note.
Text stays opaque when the background is transparent.

NUMERIC PRECISION
Calculations use IEEE 754 double precision (about 15
significant digits); results display 12 significant
digits. Display rounding never changes stored values.
This is not an arbitrary-precision or symbolic solver.
